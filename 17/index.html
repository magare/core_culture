Entry 047 Monday · 05:31 Weather: dark, then bright Wing: iron room

Arrived at the iron room before the birds.

The lights were already on. Coach V was chalking a barbell like it was a ceremony. Twelve racks, all quiet, all waiting. By six there were nine of us lifting and nobody had said a full sentence — the good kind of quiet, the kind with intent underneath it.

Entry 048 Thursday · 19:44 Weather: neon Wing: studio, second floor

The dance floor at dusk does not count reps.

Thursday's yoga ran late so the zumba crowd warmed up in the corridor. By 19:45 the studio was pink light and hair. The instructor calls moves like weather warnings. A beginner in the corner went from suspicious to unstoppable in eleven minutes flat — logged, witnessed.

← "cardio is a byproduct" — write that down
Entry 049 Saturday · 11:12 Weather: 4°C, deliberately Wing: remedy

Notes from the cold, taken quickly.

Three minutes is a long time when the water is right. Field protocol: sit, breathe slow enough to be bored, get out before you want to. The steam room afterwards is the whole reward. Ice, it turns out, is a excellent listener and a terrible conversationalist.
