More than a gym

Core Culture was built in the weight room, but it was never only about the weight room. Training is only part of the culture — the rest is ice, steam, sofas, screens and people who wave at you by name.

One space. More than one way to move

Our strength floor fits biomechanics, not the other way around. Our engine bay runs on sleds, assault bikes and long intervals. The studio hosts dance, yoga and Zumba for people who train — and for people who just like moving to music.

Recovery is training too

Cold, then warm, then quiet. Ice bath, steam and sauna sit ten steps from the racks, because the session doesn't end when the weights are racked.

Train. Move. Recover. Connect.

That's the whole philosophy. Looking for the timetable, culture nights or the lounge? It's all under one roof at The Foundry District — open 05:00–23:00, seven days.
