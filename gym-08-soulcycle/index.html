The ritual

Every ride begins
before the pedals.

The room is the ritual. The bike is only where it happens — three steps, the same every time, in every studio, since the first ride.

01

Name on the board

Your bike is chalked with your name before you arrive. No screens, no sign-in queue — the room already knows you’re coming.

02

Lights down low

At the minute mark the overheads die and the candles take over. Phones go in the basket by the door. The day stays outside.

03

First pull of the match

The coach strikes the match, the front row catches it, and the ride starts in near-dark — together, from the first beat.
