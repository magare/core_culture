# 08 — ACNE STUDIOS (acnestudios.com/eu/en) — design-grammar study for Core Culture

Reference territory: avant-garde editorial design · fashion typography · unconventional spacing · deliberately unusual compositions.
Confidence: FULL (live observation, headed Chromium 1440×900, 2026-09-20; cookie dialog dims some captures). Screenshots `/tmp/acne-shots/`, facts `/tmp/acne-report.json`.

## Snapshot

Scandinavian avant-garde with a gallery hangover: a light header with 12px uppercase micro-nav (WOMAN / MAN / DENIM / BAGS / SCARVES), centered sentence-case logotype, and utility links right (SEARCH / HELP / ACCOUNT / 00). The hero is a full-bleed moody campaign image with the logotype repeated as a GIANT ghost watermark across the frame, a tiny caps eyebrow top-left ("30 YEARS OF ACNE STUDIOS") and a tiny "SHOP THE COLLECTION" bottom-left. Objects sit as cutouts on flat gray; photography is dark studio moody; a NEWS section mixes a large plain-type paragraph with small cards. The footer: gray EMAIL input, four tiny columns, a bottom row with © + two-letter socials + "SHIPPING TO SPAIN (ENGLISH)". Zero decoration, zero radius, monochrome everything.

## Visual identity (hard values)

- **BG** white; gray fields `#e8e8e8`-ish; text `#000`; footer inputs gray fill. NO accent color anywhere.
- **Type**: `Helvetica Now Text` — micro-labels **12px uppercase** everywhere (nav, eyebrows, captions); logotype sentence case medium; body paragraph ~18px regular sentence case; `Helvetica Monospaced Pro` for meta/prices.
- **Buttons/links**: underlined micro-caps ("COOKIE SETTINGS" boxed outline; "SHOP THE COLLECTION" plain); no filled CTAs on imagery.
- **Decoration**: none — the giant ghost logotype watermark IS the decoration.

## Composition

- Header: nav left / logotype center / utilities right, all 12px caps, tight 60px bar.
- Hero ~full-viewport: darkened campaign image; ghost logotype spanning edge-to-edge; eyebrow top-left; bottom-left utility link.
- Object strip: 4–5 cutouts on light gray, no captions (product-led).
- Arrivals: tiny caps label + gapless 2-up full-height moody images.
- "DISCOVER MORE" + 4-up gray tiles, object cutouts, tiny caps caption bottom-left.
- NEWS: left large plain-type paragraph; right 2 cards (image + small caption below).
- Footer: NEWSLETTER + paragraph + huge gray EMAIL input; DISCOVER OUR STORES; 4 tiny columns (CONTACT US/HELP/SHOP/COMPANY); bottom row © + IG FB TW YT PT WB + shipping note.
- Spacing: extreme; single-column logic; hairline-free (separation by whitespace and fields).

## Motion

Almost none: hovers = underline/opacity; no scroll animation. Deliberately static.

## Storytelling

Campaign → objects → arrivals → discover → news (the house's cultural happenings: Paper, books, exhibitions) → utilitarian footer. Voice: dry, institutional, cool.

## SIGNATURE CHARACTERISTICS

1. **12px uppercase micro-type everywhere** — nav, eyebrows, captions; logotype sentence case centered.
2. **Giant ghost logotype watermark** across the hero.
3. **Objects as cutouts on flat gray fields.**
4. **Moody studio photography**, desaturated, dark.
5. **Monochrome discipline** — zero accent, zero radius, zero decoration.
6. **NEWS as a large plain paragraph + small cards** — the house journal.
7. **Utilitarian footer** with gray input + tiny columns + shipping note.
