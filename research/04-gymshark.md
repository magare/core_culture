# 04 — GYMSHARK (gymshark.com) — design-grammar study for Core Culture

Reference territory: contemporary training culture · bold fitness imagery · energetic commercial composition.
Confidence: FULL (live observation, headed Chromium 1440×900, 2026-09-20; region + cookie overlays present in captures, content read around them). Screenshots `/tmp/gs-shots/`, computed facts `/tmp/gs-report.json`.

## Snapshot

Training-culture commerce with zero pretension: white page, near-black type, one green accent, and EVERYTHING UPPERCASE — display type is a bold slightly-condensed industrial grotesk ("Plaak Gymshark", measured 48px/800, line-height 0.9) over a quiet neutral body ("SN Skandia", 14-16px/400). Square corners everywhere (radius 0 on buttons, cards, chips). Price-led cards on beige/light-gray fields with a green tag badge; section heads are small (16-18px/800 UPPERCASE); energy comes from big uppercase statements on flat color fields and full-bleed 2-up halves.

## Visual identity (hard values)

- **BG** white; product fields warm light-gray/beige (#f0-ef); promo/email bands: green gradient field (#2f9d72→#67c59a, grainy); text `#0d1012` (rgb(13,16,18)); body gray `#5a5f62`; accent green ~`#37a07a` (badges/bars); region dialog near-black `#17191b`.
- **Type**: H2 "UP TO 50% OFF*" **48px / 43.2 (0.9) / 800 / uppercase**, white on green. Section heads ("WOMEN'S SALE", "POPULAR RIGHT NOW", "MORE ABOUT GYMSHARK") **16-18px / 800 / uppercase** black. Product captions **13-14px / 500 / uppercase** ("LEGGINGS FROM $22"). Body 14-16px/400 gray. Card labels under tiles sentence case 14px.
- **Buttons**: **white square** (radius 0, 16px 24px, 14px/400-600, black text) on imagery — "Shop now"; square outline chips for tabs (active = black border); underlined text links on green band ("SIGN UP").
- **Decoration**: green square tag badge top-left corner of product cards; payment icon row; thin hairlines in footer; NO radius anywhere.

## Composition

- Promo bar (green, centered small text + pause); header transparent → left nav (Women/Men/Accessories/Sale), **centered logotype**, right icons (search/heart/person/bag); white sticky on scroll.
- Hero: large green gradient field with centered uppercase stack (statement → line → smaller line), followed by **2-up tall image halves** with uppercase bold caption + white square button (Women's Sale / Men's Sale).
- Product rows: small uppercase head at ~40px margin → 4-up cards (image on beige field, green badge, caption "X FROM $Y") stacked directly (WOMEN'S SALE row then MEN'S SALE row).
- "NEW IN": full-bleed 2-up halves (image, uppercase caption + button), zero gap.
- "POPULAR RIGHT NOW": uppercase head + **two square tab chips** (Women/Men, active black-bordered) switching a 4-up card row.
- Email band: green gradient, centered uppercase statement + one line + underlined SIGN UP.
- SEO text block: uppercase bold mini-heads ("WORKOUT CLOTHES & GYM CLOTHES", "MORE THAN YOUR BEST WORKOUT CLOTHING") + gray paragraphs with bold inline links.
- Footer: 4 link columns (Help/My Account/Pages) + "More About Gymshark" 3-up gray tile cards (icon + label bottom-left: Blog / Students 12% / Email Sign Up), payment icons, legal row (underlined links) "© 2026 | Gymshark Limited | All Rights Reserved. | We Do Gym."
- Density: 4-up rows, tight vertical rhythm (~48-64px between bands), commercial repetition welcome.

## Motion

Minimal: hover states (button/underline), carousel pause on promo, no scroll animation observed. Energy = typography + imagery, not effects.

## Storytelling

Sale-first, community-backed: hero statement → category 2-up → product rows → new-in → popular w/ tabs → email capture → SEO storytelling ("Our legacy was built in the weight room…") → utilitarian footer. Voice: direct, second person, energetic ("We Do Gym").

## SIGNATURE CHARACTERISTICS

1. **Uppercase 800-weight condensed display at 0.9 leading** over tiny quiet body — loud/commercial.
2. **Square-corner discipline** — radius 0 on every button, chip, card, badge.
3. **One green accent** on white/black — promo bar, email band, card badges.
4. **Price-led product cards on beige fields** — image, green tag badge, "X FROM $Y" uppercase caption.
5. **2-up full-bleed halves with uppercase captions + white square buttons** (dual-audience pattern).
6. **Square tab chips** filtering a card row (Women/Men).
7. **Green gradient statement bands** (hero + email capture) with centered stacks.
8. Community sign-off footer ("We Do Gym.").
