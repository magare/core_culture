    The lounge

    Work-lift balance.

    A meeting before your session, a chapter of a novel after. The lounge keeps tables in the light, espresso on the machine, and Wi-Fi that behaves — so the hours around training belong to the rest of your life.

    Hobby clubs gather here weekly: dance, yoga, zumba. Fitness that doesn't have to look like lifting, for people who'd rather not take themselves too seriously.
