## Snapshot

A gallery that sells eyewear. Full-bleed surreal campaign films (fur, tomato field, a diver floating over a sheep) with a small centered serif collection title and two ghost-outline pills; thin progress dashes below. Chrome almost disappears: transparent header with a centered serif logotype, light sans UI, and flat gray `#F3F4F6` fields where products sit like museum objects with tiny name/price captions and underlined utility links ("ADD TO WISHLIST", "MORE", "PRODUCT VIEW"). The footer is a single quiet row.

## Visual identity (hard values)

- **BG** white; section fields flat `#F3F4F6`; text near-black; muted grays. No accent color — mood comes from the films.
- **Type**: campaign/brand `gentleMonsterSerif` ("2026 VEGGIE COLLECTION" 24px/400; logotype GENTLE MONSTER serif caps); UI `gentleSans` light 350-400 weight, 14-16px. Captions sentence case, tiny.
- **Buttons**: ghost pills (1px white outline, transparent, radius 999: "Shop Now" / "View Campaign") on films; underlined text links on fields (MORE, ADD TO WISHLIST).
- **Decoration**: 5 thin progress dashes under the hero; thumbnail strip + "PRODUCT VIEW" for the drop; nothing else.

## Composition

- Transparent header (left nav 5 items, CENTERED serif logotype, right: cart item label + search/account/bag).
- Hero = multi-scene campaign carousel (~95vh), centered serif title + ghost pills + dashes.
- Flat gray band with tiny head "LATEST: GENTLE MONSTER'S NEW ARRIVAL" + underlined MORE; model cutouts on the gray.
- "BEST: THIS WEEK TOP 20 / MORE" product carousel: object on gray, name (Futura 02(BRG)) + price + ADD TO WISHLIST.
- Minimal single-row footer: Contact Us · Customer Service · Store Locator · Legal Notice · Subscribe · Social · Country + © GENTLE MONSTER.
- Enormous negative space; 1 idea per screen; density zero.

## Motion

Campaign carousel crossfade with dashes; hovers = underline; otherwise still. Mysterious = withheld information + cinematic film.

## Storytelling

Collection-as-exhibition: film → latest arrival → best objects → utilitarian footer. Voice: minimal, enigmatic, object-led.

3. Thin progress dashes (hero carousel).

6. Minimal single-row footer.
7. Withheld-information storytelling (MORE links, "PRODUCT VIEW" thumbnails).
