Editor's letter

We were tired of gyms that photograph like warehouses and behave like warehouses. So we commissioned a club the way you commission an issue: strength as the cover story, dance as the fashion pages, recovery as the back-page ritual — and a lounge that reads like the letters section, because that is where everybody writes in.

— The editors, Core Culture

The weight room diaries.

Under the mezzanine, twelve racks behave like a private atelier — chalk instead of charcoal, calibrated plates instead of clay. Coached barbell hours run from first pull to competition plate, and the only thing we photograph is your progress.

Words — The floorHours — 05:30 daily
"Strength is the cover story. Everything else is editorial."

Choreography after dark.

The running report: monotony is out of season. Treadmills screen entire series; the studio runs dance, zumba and yoga under club lights until the mirrors fog. Cardio, this season, is a side effect of enjoying yourself.

Shoot — Studio B, 19:30Soundtrack — Provided
"Nobody counts beats. Everybody stays for the last song."

F·03

Cold water therapy.

The remedy wing is the quietest room in the issue: steel, ice, steam. Three minutes at four degrees, breathing slowly enough to bore yourself calm. Athletes book it like a spa appointment; the spa books it like a dare.

Location — Remedy wingTemperature — 4°C
"Recover like an athlete. Perform like one."

F·04

Main character energy.

A gym should not look like a room full of machines; it should look like the third act of a music video. Pop culture on the walls, playlists on the floor, lighting that assumes someone is about to do something worth watching.

Art — House commissionMood — Main character
"Tired of gyms that look like a room full of machines? Us too."

F·05

The lounge edit.

Work-life balance? Try work-lift balance. Meetings before the session, people after it — a lounge with unmetered coffee, strong opinions and no clocks. The workout ends; the culture does not.

Setting — The loungeDuration — As long as you like
"Got a meeting before your workout? Or work to finish after?"
