# 05 — ARC'TERYX (arcteryx.com, geo-served /ca/en) — design-grammar study for Core Culture

Reference territory: technical performance · outdoor-documentary photography · functional visual systems · product precision.
Confidence: FULL (live observation, headed Chromium 1440×900, 2026-09-20; cookie bar overlays some captures). Screenshots `/tmp/arc-shots/`, facts `/tmp/arc-report.json`.

## Snapshot

Quiet expedition authority: a black utility bar, a white ROUNDED-CORNER header card floating over a full-bleed documentary hero, and a centered campaign voice in letterspaced uppercase condensed serif (elan-itc-pro, 32px, +0.32px tracking) — flanked by outline buttons and underlined text links. Photography does the persuading (moody video, fabric macros, athletes mid-ascent); UI stays white/black/photography-color. Footer is a black "LEAVE IT BETTER" close with newsletter.

## Visual identity (hard values)

- **BG** white; utility bar near-black; header card white with ~8px radius floating over hero (margin ~16px, drop-free — separation by contrast); footer black.
- **Type**: campaign voice `elan-itc-pro` **UPPERCASE, 32px/32, weight 400, +0.32px tracking** ("INTRODUCING THE SPERRO SV", "THE ORANGE SHIRT", "LEAVE IT BETTER"); UI voice `helvetica-now-display` 16px/24 400 ("Veilance" h3 28px/400 sentence case); tile captions small caps; body 14-16px gray #4a4a4a.
- **Buttons**: **1px outline rectangles** (transparent fill, white on imagery: "Women's" / "Men's", ~48px tall, side-by-side pair); **underlined text links** ("More on System 0™", "Download", "Start your TRADE-IN") with 2px underline offset; gray filled "SIGN ME UP" in footer.
- **Decoration**: circular pause control on video hero; thin underlines as the only ruling; NO badges, NO cards on white, NO shadows.

## Composition

- Black utility bar (category shortcuts left, center promo with caret, store-finder + region right).
- White header card floating over hero (not edge-to-edge — inset ~16px, rounded, logo left, centered nav, icons right); scrolls away (not sticky).
- Hero ~86vh full-bleed documentary video: centered stack — small monogram mark → uppercase serif headline (2 lines) → 2-line subline → outline button pair → underlined link; pause circle top-right.
- 2-up image tiles (gap 8px, full-bleed edge): captions + line + underlined links centered in the UPPER third ("THE ORANGE SHIRT", "EP.7: A NEW SYSTEM").
- 3-up category cards (contained, gap 16px, portrait 3:4) — product/athlete photography.
- 2-up action tiles ("GET THE ARC'TERYX APP" / "UPGRADES MADE EASY") — captions centered, links underlined.
- 3-up value props with brand marks (ReBIRD™ / WALK GENTLY / FREE SHIPPING & RETURNS) — centered mark + one line each on white.
- Black footer: centered serif caps "LEAVE IT BETTER"; 4 link columns (HELP / MY ACCOUNT / SHOP MORE / ABOUT US); newsletter "GET YOUR WEEKLY DOSE OF ADVENTURE" + outline input + gray button; socials + region + app row; multi-row legal.
- Margins ~40px; one idea per band; generous 64-96px breathing.

## Motion

Video hero with pause; underlines on hover; no scroll-triggered animation observed. Stillness + documentary gravity.

## Storytelling

Chapter-led: introduce the hero product → stories (community, series episode) → shop categories → services (app, trade-in) → values (repair, community, shipping) → black ethical close. Voice: understated, declarative, technical.

## SIGNATURE CHARACTERISTICS

1. **Letterspaced uppercase condensed-serif campaign voice** (small size, huge authority — 32px max).
2. **Outline button pairs + underlined text links** — never filled on imagery.
3. **White rounded header card floating over the documentary hero**; black utility bar above.
4. **Documentary photography** — real locations, hands, weather; color from film not UI.
5. **Centered caption stacks in upper third of tiles**; links underlined, never boxed.
6. **Value-prop trio with brand marks** on white.
7. **Black ethical close** — serif caps statement + newsletter.
