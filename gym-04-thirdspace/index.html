The house

Not a gym.
A house of practice.

The culture is the point. The equipment is only where it happens — a floor of iron, a lane of turf, a cold room at three degrees, and time enough to use all of it properly.

Core Culture opened in the Foundry District with one idea: the city is already loud, so the house doesn't have to be. Coaches who learn your name before your numbers. Sessions that book out because they're small, not because they're scarce. Recovery treated as training, not as a treat.

Nothing here is billed as extreme. The practice is the opposite — the same movements, taken seriously, for years. That is the whole trick, and it is harder than it sounds.
