fighters.

Every round has a coach on the floor and a coach on the mic. Between them they call the shots, fix your hips, and keep the count honest.

Ray Dominguez

Head coach — on the mic

Fifteen fights, zero patience for dropped hands. Calls every round like it’s the fifth of a title bout — you’ll hear him from the street.

Yuki Tanaka

Technique coach — on the floor

Wraps, stance, hips. Yuki rebuilds your punch from the ground up in round one so round five doesn’t fall apart.

Cee Martinez

Conditioning coach — the clock

Owns rounds three and five. Runs the intervals, keeps the count, and has never once ended a round early. Not once.
